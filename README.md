# Cluestone

> *Helping Commander players make better decisions before, during, and after every game.*

![Cluestone Logo sketch](images/cluestone-logo-white.png)

---

# Purpose

Cluestone is a multi-platform companion application built specifically for **Magic: The Gathering Commander**.

The goal is to provide a tool for players to use during games, and a place to track their decks' performance holistically and at a card-by-card level, through player-input. Players can use Clusetone to:

1. Build and maintain decks.
2. Manage games while they are being played.
3. Learn from past games to improve future ones.

Most existing tools excel at one of these tasks but treat the others as separate problems. Cluestone is designed around the idea that these activities are naturally connected.

Every game should improve your understanding of a deck.

Every deck change should have historical context.

Every statistic should help answer a meaningful question.

---

# Vision Statement

Create the definitive Commander companion by combining deck management, gameplay tools, and long-term analytics into a single, intuitive experience.

---

# Mission

Enable Commander players to make informed decisions by turning gameplay into useful knowledge without disrupting the experience of playing the game.

# Core Principles

## Commander First

Cluestone is designed exclusively for Commander.

Features should embrace the unique aspects of the format - including multiplayer gameplay, commanders, commander damage, deck identity, politics, and long-term deck evolution - instead of treating Commander as just another supported format.

## Connected Experiences

Deck building, gameplay, and analytics should not exist as isolated features.

Changes made in one area should naturally influence the others.

Examples include:

* Games contributing to deck statistics.
* Deck revisions influencing future performance comparisons.
* Card-level performance informing future deck changes.

## Open Ecosystem

Commander players already use many excellent tools.

Whenever possible, Cluestone should integrate with existing applications and services rather than requiring users to abandon their current workflow.

The goal is to complement the Commander ecosystem, not replace it.

## Data With Purpose

Every piece of information collected should have a reason to exist.

The objective is not to accumulate data for its own sake, but to provide meaningful insight that helps players answer questions such as:

* Which cards consistently overperform?
* Which cards rarely contribute?
* Has this deck improved over time?
* Which opponents or archetypes create challenging matchups?
* Which version of this deck has performed the best?

## Fast During Play

The application should never become the focus of the table. Common actions should require as little interaction as possible. The best game-tracking interface is one that players barely notice.

## Local First

Players should be able to use Cluestone without depending on an internet connection. Internet connectivity should enhance the experience rather than be a requirement. Synchronization should occur seamlessly whenever possible.

## Platform Native

Cluestone should feel at home on every supported platform. Rather than simply resizing a single interface, the application should respect the conventions and strengths of:

* Web
* Android
* iPhone
* iPad

## Iterative Improvement

The application will evolve through real gameplay. Features should be informed by testing, player feedback, and observed behavior rather than assumptions alone. Users should be empowered to submit feedback and suggestions.

## Player Agency

Cluestone provides insight and recommendations, not prescriptions.

Players remain in control of their decks and decisions.

Analytics and AI should explain, suggest, and educate—not replace the player's judgment.

# Target Audience

Cluestone is intended for Commander players who:

* Maintain multiple decks.
* Regularly play with consistent groups.
* Enjoy improving and refining their decks.
* Value statistics without wanting to manually maintain spreadsheets.
* Want gameplay tools that remain unobtrusive during a match.

---

# What Cluestone Is

Cluestone is:

* A Commander deck library.
* A game companion.
* A historical record of deck evolution.
* An analytics platform.
* A long-term archive of gameplay history.

---

# What Cluestone Is Not

Cluestone is not intended to be:

* A replacement for playing Magic in person.
* A comprehensive rules engine.
* A digital tabletop.
* A marketplace.
* A social media platform.
* A general-purpose Magic application supporting every format.

---

# Measures of Success

Cluestone succeeds when players can:

* Record a game without slowing down gameplay.
* Understand how a deck has changed over months or years.
* Identify meaningful trends in deck performance.
* Make confident deck-building decisions supported by actual gameplay data.
* Enjoy using the application often enough that it becomes part of their normal Commander routine.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Cluestone aims to become the primary companion application that Commander players open before shuffling their deck, keep nearby during every game, and return to afterward to understand how their decks continue to evolve.

Rather than simply storing information, Cluestone should help players tell the story of every Commander deck they build.

A wonderful project created by a dedicated team of engineers, designers, and avid Magic players. Scryfall is the "Unofficial" but oft-used Database for Magic card reference, and has a robust API for quickly retrieving card details.
